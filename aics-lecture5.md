Lecture 5 discusses how the widespread availability of AI content is changing the way we seek and obtain information, communicate with each other, and study and learn.

### What you need to understand:

1. Deepfakes and AI disclosure
2. AI content overwhelm
3. The impacts of writing assistance
4. Correlational and causal evidence for AI impact on human learning

### Sample essay questions

*How is the use of AI changing the digital commons? Is it eroding or strengthening our information ecosystem?*

*Is AI affecting our ability to learn? Is it making us dumber, or smarter, or neither?*
